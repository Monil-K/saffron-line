Saffron Line is a one-shift night-kitchen game in plain HTML and canvas: gather ingredients from the pantry bins, chop them at the board, sear them in two pans, plate the cooked parts into a dish and serve the tickets on the rail before they walk out.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play: WASD or the arrow keys to move, E or space to use the station you are standing at, the mouse to click a station and walk to it, P to pause and M to mute.
